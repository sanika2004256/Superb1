Who We Are

Superb Mega Corporation is one of the leading business conglomerates based in India. We are engaged in diverse areas of businesses & products including instant beverages, food, agricultural equipment, and global export. From a single manufacturing unit based in Sangli, the corporation today operates renowned subsidiary brands with multiple franchise outlets, agro-facilities, and distributors across India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Netherlands, and USA.

We operate an FSSAI-certified instant premix manufacturing unit offering quick beverages, while over 40,000+ acres of farmland produce the highest grade 'Bhagwa' pomegranates with advanced sorting and global cold-chain export infrastructure.
